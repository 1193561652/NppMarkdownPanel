# NppMarkdownPanel-qt: attribution and licensing

`NppMarkdownPanel-qt` is the Qt port of the original
[NppMarkdownPanel](https://github.com/mohzy83/NppMarkdownPanel) plugin for the
Qt port of Notepad++. It is an independently maintained modified version, not
an official release of the original plugin.
